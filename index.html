Ingram US Non-Traded BDC Index

Tracks the total return of five of the largest US non-traded business development companies (BDCs), using month-end NAVs and distributions reported in SEC filings.

Total return with distributions reinvested at NAV. 2023 covers May to December. Figures for the current year are year to date. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

Risk

Non-traded BDCs value most of their loans using appraisal-based methods, which tend to adjust gradually. As a result, reported monthly returns are strongly autocorrelated and reported volatility understates the risk of the underlying assets.

We publish a de-smoothed series alongside the index, using the Geltner (1993) AR(1) adjustment, so users can see both views.

Constituents

Distribution yield: latest monthly Class I distribution, annualised, divided by NAV per share. Source: fund filings on SEC EDGAR.
